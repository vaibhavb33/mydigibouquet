# Feature Gap Analysis

**Prepared by:** Senior Product Research & Strategy Team  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Baseline Reference:** `https://digitalbouquet.org/` (live audited)  
**Competitor Benchmarks:** `digibouquet.vercel.app`, `thebouquet.me`, `digibouquet.net`, `digibouquet.space`, `digiflower.net`, `Flower Architect`, `Paperless Post`, `Kudoboard`, `Jacquie Lawson`

---

## Master Feature Comparison & Opportunity Matrix

| Domain | 1. Existing Reference Features (`digitalbouquet.org`) | 2. Competitor Features | 3. Missing Features & Weaknesses | 4. Recommended Improvements (`mydigibouquet.com`) | 5. Development Complexity | 6. Business & User Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual Fidelity & Flower Library** | 12 flat watercolor bloom heads (`Orchid, Tulip, Dahlia, Anemone, Carnation, Zinnia, Ranunculus, Sunflower, Lily, Daisy, Peony, Rose`) + monochrome Gold filter. No stems shown in picker. | `Flower Architect`: 1,700+ 3D flowers, 32 angles. `digibouquet.space`: procedural 3D blooms. `digibouquet.net`: 8 blooms + rich symbolism guides. | Flat 2D outline drawings look clip-art-like; zero color variants (e.g., pink vs white rose); zero stems/buds; mobile shows **no bloom labels** (hover-only); no search/filter. | **Photorealistic studio-lit botanical assets** (28 cohesive V1 SKUs across Focal, Filler, Foliage with colorways), diffused overhead-front lighting, shadow-free decontaminated alpha WebP (`3-tier LODs`), always-visible mobile labels, color/occasion/meaning filters. | **High** *(unified lighting, edge decontamination, 3-tier LOD WebP pipeline)* | **Very High** — Primary visual moat & immediate quality leap over all free 2D clones. |
| **Greenery, Containers & Wrapping** | 4 fixed greenery+bow bundles (`Sage, Olive, Eucalyptus, Baby's Breath`) in Step 2 + 5 paper wraps in Step 4 (`Blush, Kraft, Lilac, Mist, Sage`). | `Flower Architect`: separate foliage, ribbons, vases, containers. `digibouquet.space`: dedicated wrap & greens steps. | Ribbon and foliage are locked together; paper wrap doesn't appear during arrangement (only on Step 5!); Gold flowers clash on neon-green bow; **zero vases or baskets**. | Decouple **Foliage**, **Wrapping/Vessel** (linen/kraft paper OR ceramic/terracotta/clear glass vase with interior stem clipping & glass refraction), and **Ribbon** with live Step-1 preview. | **High** *(opaque pocket alpha masking + Clear Glass Vase stem clipping & specular layering)* | **High** — Dramatically increases visual cohesion & personalization pride. |
| **Bouquet Composition & Studio Engine** | Step 3 (`BOUQUET` / `SHAPE`) has only `Try a new arrangement` (random scatter) and `Change greenery`. Confirmed **non-draggable**. | `Flower Architect`: full drag, rotate, scale, 3D angle, layering, shape guides. `digiflower.net`: real-time split-pane live preview. | Zero manual control—overlapping blooms cover each other randomly; **zero bouquet shape presets** despite mobile label `"SHAPE"`; no undo/redo; no split-pane live canvas. | **Hybrid Canvas Scene-Graph Engine**: Auto-arrange smart templates (*Classic Round, Airy Garden, Cascading, Minimalist*) **PLUS** pixel-alpha hit-testing, drag-and-drop, contextual stem inspector (rotate/scale/layer/flip), 3-tier dynamic canvas shadows, & Undo/Redo. | **Very High** *(Konva/Canvas scene graph, alpha hit-testing, collision-free auto-layout, touch ergonomics)* | **Very High** — Converts passive lottery clicking into genuine creative expression. |
| **Greeting Card & Stationery Editor** | 6 mismatched envelope SVGs; 1 lined cream notepad; 1 blue handwriting font; 10-line message cap; Recipient & Sender inputs. | `Paperless Post`: designer card stock, custom typography, envelope liners, wax seals, stamps. `digibouquet.net`: card mood presets. | Zero card paper styles, zero font/ink selectors, zero envelope liner or wax seal customization, no prompt helpers for writer's block. | **Tactile Stationery Suite**: Cohesive card papers (cotton, deckle-edge, botanical border, dark velvet), 4–5 curated font pairings, customizable envelope + monogram wax seal, and occasion message prompt starters. | **Medium** *(CSS/Canvas card renderer + typography system)* | **High** — Elevates emotional resonance to match `Paperless Post` without coin fees. |
| **Rich Media & Audio Experience** | Photo upload (up to 5) & Voice recording locked behind Clerk auth + "premium share credits"; YouTube embed (free); 6 music tracks with broken mobile icons. | `Kudoboard`: free/easy photo/GIF/video boards + group signing. `digibouquet.space`: lossless studio soundscapes. `Jacquie Lawson`: synced music + motion. | Broken promise between homepage ("Add photos & voice") and login/credit paywall; arcade music buttons clash visually and vanish on mobile (`390×844`). | Clean, unified acoustic audio player (working identically on desktop & mobile); frictionless **Polaroid photo frame** + short browser voice note (without ad clutter); optional **Collaborative Group Mode** ("invite friends to sign"). | **Medium–High** *(MediaRecorder API + object storage + clean audio visualizer)* | **Very High** — Unlocks viral birthdays, anniversaries, & group celebrations. |
| **Image / Video Export** | `EXPORT GIF` only on Step 5. | `Canva`: PNG/JPG/PDF/MP4. `Flower Architect`: PDF recipe + PNG. `Kudoboard`: physical poster/book print. | No crisp static image export (`PNG`/`JPG`), no mobile lock-screen wallpaper (`9:16`), no Instagram/TikTok Story card, no printable card PDF. | **Deterministic Multi-Format Exporter**: High-res **PNG/JPG** keepsake (`1:1`), **9:16 Story/Wallpaper** card (with subtle toggleable domain colophon), and **4:5 Printable Bouquet + Card** using shared text-wrapping metrics. | **Medium–High** *(Canvas 2D / offscreen render compositing at 2×–3× DPR + DOM/Canvas font parity)* | **High** — Massive organic social sharing driver (Instagram Stories, WhatsApp Status). |
| **Sharing, Privacy & Unboxing UX** | Short URL (`/share/:slug`), Copy button, QR code modal. Envelope opens halfway, clipping sender signature; leaks private note in `og:description`; static `og:image`. | `digibouquet.space`: Open-tracking ("Echo"), scheduled delivery, emoji reactions. `thebouquet.me`: public gallery. `Paperless Post`: RSVP tracking. | Sender-centric headline (*"Send a Digibouquet..."*) on recipient page; poor tap affordance; **OG metadata leaks private message before opening**; no thank-you reaction loop. | **Privacy-Safe Dynamic OG Cards** (Node `@napi-rs/canvas`/`sharp` pre-rendered `1200×630` JPEG/PNG `<300 KB`, zero note leakage, 12-char base62 slugs); **Cinematic Unboxing Reveal** + **"Send a Bouquet Back"** reply CTA. | **Medium–High** *(Node server OG pre-render/cache + Web Share API + reaction endpoint)* | **Very High** — Fixes major privacy bug & turns every recipient into a sender. |
| **Monetization, Ads & Trust** | Intrusive Google AdSense banners, inline ad chips inside studio copy, bottom sticky ads covering content, plus hidden credit paywall. | `digibouquet.vercel.app` & `digibouquet.net` & `digiflower.net`: 100% ad-free core builder. `digibouquet.space`: transparent free tier + $1–$5 upgrades. | Ads destroy emotional luxury and cause visual/click interference (especially on mobile and gallery pages). | **Zero Display Ads** inside the studio and recipient unboxing experience. Core creation, customization, image export, and sharing remain 100% free & zero-login; optional transparent supporter/premium keepsake upgrades. | **Low** *(Product & architecture discipline)* | **Very High** — Immediate trust and brand-equity advantage over `digitalbouquet.org`. |
| **Mobile Responsiveness & Performance** | Mobile stepper clips `"5 SHARE"` (`SHAF`); hidden flower labels; invisible music icons; lazy images below fold on homepage fail to load cleanly. | `digiflower.net` & `thebouquet.me`: mobile-friendly touch flows. `Jacquie Lawson`: heavy mobile animation bugs. | Severe responsive polish bugs across `390×844` mobile screens during the exact flow most recipients/senders use (texting/WhatsApp). | **Mobile-First Bottom-Sheet Studio** (plus Portrait Tablet adaptive stage, collapsible Step-3 keyboard peek, auto-saved local drafts), explicit bloom titles, touch drag + precision inspector, and `<2.0s` LCP across `375px–1440px`. | **Medium–High** *(Responsive Tailwind layout, Konva touch stage, localStorage draft persistence)* | **Critical** — >70% of shared gift links open on mobile messaging apps. |
| **SEO & Occasion Discovery** | 6 gallery mood pages + `/virtual-cake` + `/gallery/photobooth-online-girlfriend`, cluttered by AdSense. | `digibouquet.net`: deep floriography guides, message ideas, editorial trust pages. `digiflower.net`: FAQ schema + multilingual occasion pages. | No dedicated occasion builders/landing pages for high-intent seasonal searches (*Mother's Day, Sympathy, Graduation, Anniversary*) or interactive floriography encyclopedia. | **Clean Content-to-Studio Funnel**: Dedicated SEO occasion landing pages (`/occasions/birthday`, `/occasions/apology`, etc.) + interactive **Flower Meanings Encyclopedia** (`/flowers/[slug]`) with 1-click *"Build with this flower"* preloaded presets. | **Medium** *(SSG/ISR Next.js pages + JSON-LD schema)* | **High** — Compounding organic acquisition engine without ad-network clutter. |
