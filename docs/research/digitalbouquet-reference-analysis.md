## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive technical, aesthetic, and legal inspection of **digitalbouquet.org** (and related virtual flower platforms) in response to the design directive:
> *"Completely replace the cartoon-like flower rendering system with a professional, photorealistic floral library."*

While digitalbouquet.org popularized casual virtual floral gifting, its visual assets rely on 2D watercolor illustrations, stylized flat vectors, and rigid pre-stacked layering. This analysis catalogs the reference site's asset ecosystem, audits its intellectual property and licensing restrictions, and defines the technical pipeline that elevates **Petal & Prose** into an authentic, florist-grade digital botanical atelier.

### 2.2 Visual Style & Mechanics Breakdown
1. **Illustration Style:** All stems on digitalbouquet.org are rendered in a **hand-painted watercolor / flat illustrated aesthetic**. While charming for informal greetings, the assets lack photorealism, subsurface light scattering, true petal veining, and realistic depth.
2. **Fixed Anchor Stacking:** Stems are inserted into fixed pre-calculated slots or static bush clusters. There is no true depth occlusion, no individual stem tilting around a hand-tied binding point, and no interactive collision detection.
3. **Wrapping & Stationery:** Bouquet wraps are stylized trapezoidal pastel blocks; cards are displayed as flat rectangular cards with modern system fonts.

## 3. Intellectual Property, Copyright & Licensing Audit

### 3.1 Legal Analysis of Reference Website Assets
- **Proprietary Copyright:** The visual artwork, illustrations, icons, and codebase hosted on `digitalbouquet.org` are proprietary intellectual property owned by its creators.
- **No Open-Source or CC License:** The platform does not license its graphics under Creative Commons (CC-BY, CC0) or commercial open-source licenses. Scraping, extracting, or hotlinking these assets would constitute copyright infringement.
- **Fair Use Inapplicability:** Directly copying raster or vector assets from a competing web service for use in a production platform does not meet Fair Use criteria.

### 3.2 Strategic Decision: 100% Independent Photographic Creation
To ensure absolute legal compliance, professional brand distinction, and superior graphical fidelity:
1. **Zero Scraped Assets:** No assets, SVGs, or rasters are taken from digitalbouquet.org or third-party web scrapers.
2. **Independent Production Pipeline:** Every flower, leaf, petal, wrap, vase, and ribbon in Petal & Prose is custom-produced through our high-resolution studio botanical photography pipeline.
3. **Proprietary Studio License:** All assets are cataloged under the *Petal & Prose Proprietary Photographic Studio License*, granting unrestricted worldwide deployment rights across our web application, print exports, and open-graph previews.
